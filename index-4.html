CRUISE Civitavecchia Port
Port ↔ apartment

Best value

Reach Civitavecchia railway station and take a Trenitalia regional train into Rome, then continue by taxi or public transport. Leave generous time on embarkation days.

TRAIN Roma Termini

Take Metro A toward Battistini → Barberini, then walk to Vicolo Scanderbeg 43. Alternatively, use the official taxi rank outside Termini.

While you’re staying in my home

A few things I ask

This is my private home, not a hotel, and there is no reception on site. I care deeply about the house and about my neighbours. Please treat the space with the same care you would your own. If anything breaks or does not work properly, tell me as soon as possible so I can help.

Taking care of my home
Please do not damage furniture or objects or use them improperly.
Please be careful with the walls, especially when moving luggage.
Please avoid staining bed linen and towels.
When you go out, please turn off heating / A/C and lights.
Do not disconnect the Wi-Fi router, external security camera or any cables behind the sofa.
Guests & neighbours
Only registered guests may enter the property, as required by Italian law.
Parties and events are strictly forbidden.
Please respect the tranquillity of the condominium, quiet hours and common areas.
Always close the apartment/building doors and gate, and do not let unknown people enter.
Do not put dirty dishes or cutlery back into cabinets or drawers.
Please do not leave food around the house, as it can attract ants.
Plumbing · very important
Do not put food or solids down any drain; please dispose of them properly.
Only toilet paper may be flushed. No wipes — even “flushable” ones — cotton balls, dental floss, kitchen paper, facial tissues, condoms or sanitary products. Please use the bins provided.

If a blockage is caused by improper use, the plumber service will be charged.

Keys & locks

Please never force a key in a lock. If it does not turn, check that you have the correct key and direction, then try gently. Forcing a key can break it inside the lock.

Locksmith service will be charged if damage is caused by misuse.

Good to know

Rome & Vatican essentials

Vatican City

St Peter's Basilica: approximately 30–35 minutes on foot from the apartment, or walk to Barberini and take Metro A toward Battistini to Ottaviano, then continue on foot.

Vatican Museums: Metro A Barberini → Ottaviano, followed by a walk. For museum tickets, use the official Vatican Museums website whenever possible.

Official Vatican Museums
💳 Cards & cash

Cards and contactless payments are widely used. A little euro cash can still be convenient.

🔌 Electricity

Italy uses 230 V / 50 Hz. Check that US appliances support 230 V; a plug adapter alone does not convert voltage.

💧 Water

Rome's public “nasoni” fountains provide drinking water; a refillable bottle is useful.

🍽 Tipping

US-style 20% tipping is not expected. Rounding up or leaving a modest extra amount for good service is optional and appreciated.

👜 Crowds

At stations, on public transport and around major attractions, keep phones and wallets secure and bags closed.

⛪ Churches

Dress respectfully; major religious sites may enforce clothing and security requirements.
